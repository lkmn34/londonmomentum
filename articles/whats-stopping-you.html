What’s stopping you? 6 barriers and how to beat them

No time, no money, too old, the kids, boredom or nerves – a realistic fix for each.

Beat the Barriers⏱ 5 min read✔ Fact-checked against UK sources

Around 1 in 4 adults in England do less than 30 minutes of physical activity a week – and in London, about a third of adults aren’t meeting the recommended levels. Most of us have a reason. Here are the six we hear most, and a fix for each.

⏳ “I don’t have time.”

Every minute counts. Activity of any length adds up towards your 150 minutes – take the stairs, walk part of your commute, get out at lunchtime.

💷 “I can’t afford a gym.”

Move for free. Brisk walks, the free NHS Couch to 5K app, parkrun, home workouts and your local park cost nothing.

👵 “I’m too old to start.”

It’s never too late. Start gently and build up. Over 65? Add strength and balance activities on 2 days a week.

🧒 “I’ve got the kids with me.”

Make it family time. Walk or scoot to school, play in the park, have a kitchen disco – kids copy what they see.

🥱 “I just don’t enjoy exercise.”

Find your thing. It doesn’t have to be the gym – dance, garden, cycle, swim or walk with a friend.

😰 “I feel nervous and out of place.”

Start small, bring a buddy. Step a little outside your comfort zone at a time. Health worries? Ask your GP.

Your move: pick one barrier and try one fix this week.
