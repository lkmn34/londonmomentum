New to exercise or living with a health condition? That’s exactly who these classes are for – we’ll ask a few quick health questions so your coach can keep you safe.

Good to know

💻 What you need

A phone, tablet or laptop, a bit of floor space and a bottle of water. No equipment needed for most classes.

🩺 Your safety first

If you have a health condition, check with your GP before starting. Stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.

🇬🇧 UK time

All sessions run on UK time (GMT, or BST in summer), so they fit around London working days.
