Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a preview version and will be reviewed before online booking and newsletter sign-up go live.

Who we are

London Momentum is a health and fitness community based in London, UK. We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data stored on your device

Our tools, the daily check-in and preview booking requests save your answers in your own browser’s local storage so they’re there next time you visit. This information is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time from the tools page or your browser settings.

When booking and the newsletter go live

We’ll collect only what we need (such as your name, email and the health information you choose to give for class safety), explain why, keep it secure and never sell it. Health information is special category data and will only be used with your explicit consent.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
