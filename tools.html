Free health tools

Private, quick and based on UK guidance. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

BMI calculator

Body mass index for adults aged 18 and over.

Source: NICE guideline NG246 – Overweight and obesity management · NHS – BMI healthy weight calculator

Waist-to-height ratio

NICE advises keeping your waist to less than half your height.

How to measure: find the bottom of your ribs and the top of your hips. Wrap a tape measure around your waist midway between these points (just above the belly button) and breathe out naturally before measuring. Use the same units (cm or inches) for both.

Source: NICE guideline NG246 – Overweight and obesity management

Weekly activity tracker

Add your minutes for each day this week (Monday to Sunday). Moderate = you can talk but not sing. Vigorous = you can’t say more than a few words.

Source: UK Chief Medical Officers’ physical activity guidelines · NHS – Physical activity guidelines for adults aged 19 to 64

Alcohol unit counter

Add what you drink in a typical week.

Source: NHS – Calculating alcohol units

Water tracker

Aim for 6 to 8 cups or glasses of fluid a day – more when it’s hot or you’re active. Water, lower-fat milk and sugar-free drinks including tea and coffee all count.

Source: NHS – Water, drinks and your health

5 A Day counter

One adult portion is 80g of fruit or veg – roughly a handful. Fresh, frozen, tinned and dried all count; fruit juice counts once a day at most.

Source: NHS – 5 A Day

These tools give general information, not a diagnosis. If you’re worried about your health, speak to your GP. Water and 5 A Day reset each day.
